G) Economics (business-first, non-technical)

Section title

Predictable economics that scale with usage

Body

INFRA is designed around a simple principle: network activity produces measurable economic output. Every transaction generates a fixed 10 AED fee, denominated in Dirham, creating a transparent and forecastable revenue base that grows with real usage—not market cycles.

Subsection: “How fees are used”

Transaction fees are routed into predefined allocations that support:
	•	Network operation and long-term sustainability
	•	Validator incentives
	•	Dividend and incentive pools
	•	Business and developer programs
	•	Consumer growth incentives, including a referral mechanism (“Infinity Rewards”)

Short note on Infinity Rewards (business-safe wording)

Infinity Rewards is a built-in referral mechanism that distributes a small, decreasing share of certain fees to referrers. Rewards come from real fees—not token inflation—aligning growth with real activity.

J) Technical section (single section, summary only)

Section title

Technology overview (summary)

One-paragraph summary

INFRA is implemented as an EVM-compatible blockchain network using a Proof of Staked Authority model with a curated validator set, designed for fast confirmation and stable operation. Validator infrastructure is secured using a sentry-node architecture, and the network provides standard RPC endpoints, a block explorer, monitoring, and operational controls suitable for production environments.

4 technical bullets (still readable)
	•	Fast blocks: ~3 second block time and high throughput parameters  ￼
	•	Fixed fee enforcement: 10 AED per transaction is enforced by the protocol, not by gas bidding
	•	Stable mint/burn model: reAED is minted when collateral is deposited and burned on redemption  ￼
	•	Production stack: RPC, explorer, monitoring/alerting, backups, and defined ops roles

Regulatory and risk alignment
